Overview

You can request deletion of your Running Tracker Partner (PacePilot) account and associated cloud data using the form below or by emailing edwardsundarsingh12@gmail.com.

Submitting a request does not delete your account or data immediately. Each request is reviewed and processed manually by the developer after confirming it comes from the account owner. You'll receive an email when deletion is complete.

You only have a cloud account if you signed in with Google in the app (Settings → Account & backup). If you never signed in, all your data is on your phone only — see Data on your phone.

What data is deleted

A deletion request covers, as applicable:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account information for Running Tracker Partner (Google account ID, email address and display name).
  • Cloud-backed running history and run summaries, including splits, best efforts, records and notes.
  • GPS route data stored in the cloud.
  • Other personal data linked to your account, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Data on your phone

Runs stored locally on a device stay on that device until you delete them or uninstall the app. You can delete individual runs from a run's summary screen at any time. Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud data — use this page to request that.

What may be kept

  • Records of Google Play purchases (product, purchase time and a hashed purchase token) may be retained where required for billing, tax, fraud-prevention or legal purposes. Payment details are never held by us — they're managed by Google Play.
  • The email conversation about your request is kept as a record that the request was handled.
  • Data held by Google itself (your Google account, Google Play purchase history, AdMob advertising data) is controlled by Google and managed in your Google account settings.

What happens next

  • We receive your request by email.
  • We reply to the account email to confirm the request came from you. We'll never ask for your password.
  • After confirmation we delete the account data listed above and email you once it's done.
