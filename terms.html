Health & safety

The app is a training aid, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting or changing an exercise program. Always stay aware of traffic and your surroundings — don't look at your phone where it is unsafe to do so. You run at your own risk.

Accuracy

Pace, distance, elevation, calories, personal records and PR-pace predictions are estimated from GPS and device sensors. They can vary with signal quality, hardware and environment and are not guaranteed to be exact.

Pro & billing

Running Tracker Partner Pro is available through Google Play as:

  • Monthly — $0.99 per month, auto-renewing.
  • Yearly — $2.99 per year, auto-renewing.
  • Lifetime — $4.99, one-time purchase.

Prices shown in Google Play in your local currency apply. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, and you keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy. A Google sign-in is required to buy Pro so that it can be restored on your other devices.

Pro removes ads and unlocks the full best-effort table on every run. Features that are free — tracking, records, voice coach, backup and export — remain free.

First-launch trial

New installs receive a one-time, 10-minute trial of Pro features. It requires no payment or sign-in, does not renew and does not convert into a subscription. Nothing recorded during the trial is removed when it ends.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad and offers optional rewarded ads, served by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown on the live run screen.

Account & backup

Signing in with Google is optional. Backup is provided as-is; keep an exported copy of your runs if they matter to you. See the Privacy Policy for how this data is handled.
